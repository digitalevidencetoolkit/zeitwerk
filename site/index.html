Zeitwerk
About Code Data

Zeitwerk timestamps are signed by independent witnesses. Every hour, each one seals the SHA-256 fingerprints it was sent into one tree, signs the root, and publishes the whole list to its own public record.

Each witness signs its own hours, so a fingerprint sent to several is attested by each. Checking that they agree is the next piece.

Witnesses

Mirrors

Copies of a witness's record, kept by someone else. Any copy is enough to verify a timestamp, so each one makes the record harder to lose.
